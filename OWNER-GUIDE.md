# City Florist: Owner's Guide

Your website shows your flowers, bouquets and decorations, and sends every order to your WhatsApp. Your **dashboard** is where you add products, change prices, run offers and see orders.

## Opening the dashboard

- On the website, tap the **person icon** at the top right (on a phone: the **menu** button, then **Sign in**).
- Or go straight to **yourdomain.com/admin**. Bookmark it on your phone.

You can log in with your **username, email or mobile number**, plus your password. Tap the **eye** to see what you typed. Tick **Remember me** only on your own phone.

**The very first time,** the dashboard asks you to choose your own password. Pick one you don't use anywhere else: at least 8 characters, ideally a short sentence like `RosesOpenAt8am!`.

## Turn on two-step login (do this once, takes 2 minutes)

This means someone who learns your password still can't get in without your phone.

1. On your phone, install **Google Authenticator** from the Play Store or App Store.
2. In the dashboard: **Settings → Two-step login → Turn on two-step login**, and type your password.
3. In the app, tap **+** → **Scan a QR code** and point the camera at the code on the screen.
4. Type the 6-digit number the app shows, then tap **Turn on**.
5. You'll see **10 backup codes**. Tap **Download as a file**, or write them on paper and keep them somewhere safe at home or in the shop. Each one lets you log in once if you lose your phone.

From now on, after your password, open the app and type the 6-digit code it shows for City Florist. The code changes every 30 seconds.

## Everyday tasks

| To do this | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Add a new product | **Products → + Add product**: name, category, price, photos straight from your phone |
| Change many prices at once | **Products**, type the new prices in the list, then **Save all changes** |
| Run today's discount | **Daily Offers**: choose the %, what it's for (everything, one category or one product), and **Today only**. It switches off by itself |
| Choose today's ready bouquets | **Products → Flower Bouquets**, tick **Ready to go** on 4–6 bouquets, **Save all changes** |
| Hide a product for now | Open it and untick **Visible on website** |
| See and answer orders | **Orders**: tap **WhatsApp** to reply, then set the status (Contacted, Done…) |
| Change shop phone, hours, address, map | **Settings → Shop details** |

**Reviews:** customers can write a review on the home page. It appears on the website only after you press **Show on website** in **Reviews** (the number on the sidebar shows how many are waiting). Approve honest reviews, good or bad; answer unhappy customers on WhatsApp.

**Getting found on Google:** when people search "bouquet shop in Hubballi" or "flower decoration near me", Google mostly shows shops by their **Google reviews**. After every delivery, set the order to **Delivered**, then tap **★ Ask for review**: it opens WhatsApp with a thank-you message and the link to your Google review page. A few new Google reviews every week help more than anything else. Never write reviews yourself or pay for them; Google removes them and can hide the shop.

**Prices:** a product at ₹0 shows "Ask for price". Wholesale flowers never show a price, because market rates change daily, so customers message you.

**Orders:** when a customer taps **Buy**, their name, phone and the product arrive in your WhatsApp, and the order is saved in **Orders** even if they never press Send. The price always comes from your dashboard; customers can't change it.

## Policies on the website

The footer links to a **Privacy Policy** and **Delivery & Refund Terms**, and the order form links to both. They describe how the shop works today: orders confirmed on WhatsApp, payment arranged on WhatsApp, delivery in Hubballi, free cancellation before preparation starts, and problems reported on the same day with a photo. **Read them once** and tell your developer if any rule should be different (for example, how much notice cakes need, or how refunds are paid).

## Staff and customer accounts

- **Staff:** a staff member opens the dashboard login and taps **Create a staff account**. Nothing opens for them until you approve it in **Settings → Team** (a number on Settings shows how many are waiting). Staff can manage products, offers, orders and reviews; only you can change shop details, categories and the team. Use **Switch off** or **Remove** when someone leaves.
- **Customers:** customers can create an account on the website (person icon at the top right). Their name and phone are filled in when they order, and they can see their orders under **My account**. Orders from accounts appear in **Orders** like any other.

## Staying safe

- **Check "Login before this one"** in **Settings → Login details** now and then. If it shows a time you didn't log in, change your password straight away. That also logs out every other phone or computer.
- Never share your password or the 6-digit codes, even with someone who says they're "from the website" or "from Google". Nobody genuine will ask.
- Log out (top-right picture → **Log out**) when you use a shared or shop computer.
- Save your **email** in **Settings → Login details**, so "Forgot password?" can send you a reset link.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Forgot password | On the login page, tap **Forgot password?** and enter the email saved in Login details. The link works for 60 minutes |
| Lost or changed phone | Log in with one of your **backup codes**, then **Settings → Two-step login → Turn off** and turn it on again with the new phone |
| No backup codes and no phone | Contact your website developer. They can switch two-step login off on the server |
| "Too many attempts" | Wait 15 minutes and try again. This lock stops strangers guessing your password |
| Website shows old prices | Refresh the page. Changes show immediately; your phone may just be showing an old copy |
| A photo won't upload | Use JPG, PNG or WebP photos, up to about 20 MB each. Try fewer photos at a time |
